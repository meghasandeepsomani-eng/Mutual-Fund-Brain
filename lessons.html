Mutual Fund Lessons

Lesson 1: What is Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund pools money from many investors and invests in shares, bonds and securities.

Lesson 2: What is NAV?

NAV means Net Asset Value. It is the price of one unit of mutual fund.

Lesson 3: What is SIP?

SIP means Systematic Investment Plan where investor invests fixed amount regularly.

Lesson 4: Types of Mutual Funds

  • Equity Funds
  • Debt Funds
  • Hybrid Funds
  • Index Funds

Lesson 5: Risk and Return

Higher returns generally come with higher risk.
