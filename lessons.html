What is Saving?

Saving refers to the process of setting aside a portion of present income for future use. The primary objective of saving is safety and emergency availability of money.

Common saving avenues include savings accounts, fixed deposits, recurring deposits and cash reserves.

Let’s Revise

Saving means keeping money safely for future use.

  • Safety
  • Liquidity
  • Emergency availability

The Examiner’s Lens

Saving is the first step towards investment because only saved money can be invested.

What is Investing?

Investment means allocating money into assets with the objective of wealth creation.

Investments are commonly made in shares, mutual funds, bonds, gold and real estate.

Let’s Revise

Investing means using money today to create more money in future.

  • Wealth creation
  • Inflation protection
  • Future growth

The Examiner’s Lens

Investment helps in wealth creation and achieving long-term financial goals.
