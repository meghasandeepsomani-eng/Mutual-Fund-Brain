What is Saving?

Saving refers to the process of setting aside a portion of present income for future use. The primary objective of saving is not to earn very high returns, but to ensure safety and availability of money whenever required.

Saving is considered the first stage of financial planning because investment can only happen after money has been saved.

Let’s Revise

Saving means keeping money safely for future use.

  • Safety
  • Liquidity
  • Emergency availability

What is Investing?

Investment refers to the process of allocating money into financial assets for generating future income and wealth.

Investments are commonly made in shares, mutual funds, bonds, gold and real estate.

Let’s Revise

Investing means using money today to create more money in future.

  • Wealth creation
  • Future growth
  • Inflation protection
